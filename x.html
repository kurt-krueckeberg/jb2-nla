20 Nov 2026 · Kurt Krueckeberg

The project now lives in jb2-nla (Jupyter Book 2), and its navigation is the toc: section of myst.yml. That toc is a near-literal port of the old _toc.yml, so the seven Rentkammer case-file folders are still consistent only at the top level, and folder 139 is still the best model. The end goal is a printed book made from the Typst PDF, written for readers of the Krückeberg family history, so every recommendation here must work on paper as well as on the website, and the wording of the Bückeburg archivists and the Rentkammer clerks is quoted, never rewritten. Nothing in the repository was changed for this review.

Navigation consistency

In myst.yml all seven folders start with index.md → cover.md → content.md; 188 has no content.md because it has no Designatio Actorum. Below that level they diverge:

  • Document titles in myst.yml: none carry dates. Many entries have no title: (1237/doc6, 1291/doc13, 18 and 21, eight in 689, all of 188), so the sidebar falls back to the page’s long heading.

  • Order: 146 still puts two analysis pages ahead of Document 1, and 689 still puts amt-decree.md ahead of Document 1.

    This analysis should really be removed and made part the genealogical analysis process.

  • Pages not in the toc: 18 pages are not listed in myst.yml: 13 in 689, 4 in 188 and 1 in 1237 (doc4-extra.md; actors.md has been deleted).

Best folder: 139

The index.md of 139 is still the landing page that best tells a layman, in order, what the file is about, who is involved, the outcome and the timeline. It opens with two plain paragraphs (the widow’s 1824 petition and the 1849 sale before emigration), then a facts table, then a dated timeline. Its document pages also carry dates in their headings and nearly all open with a summary. Since the first review, 147 has gained a landing page of the same kind, which adds two sections worth copying everywhere: Genealogical Significance and a section pointing to the map.

Recommendations

Each folder should reach the 139 pattern: a landing page with a plain overview, a facts table, a timeline and a closing Genealogical Significance section, followed by cover, contents list, documents, later documents and supplementary notes. Everything below uses features built into MyST; none needs a plugin or custom CSS.

Titles and headings

Every page gets a short heading written for family readers. The archivists’ or clerks’ wording follows directly beneath it, in your translation, unchanged, and labelled in plain English. The heading is what prints as the chapter or section title in the PDF; the title: lines in myst.yml label only the website sidebar and should repeat the short headings.

A case-file landing page, using 1291:

# Krückeberg No. 10 Berenbusch: Forest Allotment (1817–1818)

*Archive's title:* [your translation of the Bückeburg archive's title, unchanged]

*Call number:* NLA BU, K 2, A Nr. 1291

A document page:

# Krückeberg's Petition (1 Jan. 1817)

*Listed in the case file's contents as:* Petition of the shoemaker Krückeberg No. 10 in
Berenbusch.

The Latin term “Designatio Actorum” is explained once, on the Case Files Explained page, and plain-English labels are used everywhere else.

Document pages

Keep Translation and Transliteration as two sections one after the other, as they are now. Do not use tabs or dropdowns: a printed page cannot show hidden text, and the book needs both texts visible. Replace the per-page glossaries with one glossary in rentkammer/terms.md, using MyST’s {glossary} directive and {term} role.

Every explanation of the Rentkammer and of the Vormundschaftliche Kammer (the “Guardianship” or “Regency Chamber”) belongs in one place, the “Rentkammer Explained” section under “Rentkammer Case Files”. Three pages currently carry such an explanation: 1237/index.md, 1237/doc4-extra.md and 689/index.md. Move each explanation there, and leave a one-line link to it in the page it came from.

  2. 147: apply the new heading. The landing page is otherwise done.

  4. 689: move the explanation of the “Guardianship Chamber” or “Regency Chamber” in index.md to “Rentkammer Explained”, and replace the opening with two paragraphs on the debt to Hempel & Söhne and the 200 Reichsthaler loan. Analysis to remove from the book

The book presents the documents and what they show. Research analysis belongs with the genealogical research, not in the case files. Besides the two 146 analysis pages, doc1-analysis.md and doc1-2nd-analysis.md, the following are analysis rather than presentation.

Inside the book, to remove:

  1. The argument on the 146 landing page, 146/index.md. The whole page argues that the Jobst Heinrich of 1808 is not the Jobst Heinrich of No. 10, and it ends in a note headed “Bottom line” in chatbot style (“Yes, Petzen was the parish for Evesen. No, that does not guarantee…”). The conclusion is the most important genealogical finding of the case file, so keep it in two or three sentences in a Genealogical Significance section: the 1808 petitioner was a tailor and Einlieger from Evesen without a holding, so he cannot be the shoemaker who already held No. 10 in 1798. Remove the argument itself. The page also has two typos: “(17665-18222)” and “Berebusch”.
  2. “What this Document Tells Us about Jobst Heinrich’s Status” in 146/doc6.md (lines 97–153). It is the same kind of analysis, ending in a “Bottom line” list of ✔ points. One sentence in the page’s Summary is enough.

Inside the book, to keep: the “Essential Explanations” in 146/doc6.md (Michaelmas, Meyerbrief, the annual dues). They explain terms in the document for the reader, which is presentation rather than analysis, and could later move into the glossary.

Outside the book (not listed in myst.yml): twelve pages in 689 are research working files: analyze.md, docs-1-and-2-analysis.md, other-numbering-notes.md, rescript-explained.md, workflow.md, document-markings.md, literal-doc-index.md, event-log.md, adoc1.md, adoc2.md, roster.md and glossary.md. They don’t appear in the book, but they belong with the research notes, not in the case-file folder. Two are worth mining first: glossary.md for the book’s glossary, and roster.md for a “principal persons” row on the 689 landing page.

The same goes for the repository’s own working files, claude-case-file-analysis.md, todo.md, jb2-conversions-todo.md and 188/todo.md, though none of them is in the book either.
